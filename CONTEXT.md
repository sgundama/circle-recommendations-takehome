# Circle Recommendations

Recommends Circles for a Member to join, and explains each Recommendation in
member-facing language.

## Language

### Core

**Member**:
A person with an account on Lean In Connect. The subject of a recommendation.
_Avoid_: User, account, profile

**Circle**:
A small peer group that supports Members in their careers.
_Avoid_: Group, community, network

**Membership**:
The link between a Member and a Circle they belong to, with a role of member or leader.

**Leader**:
A Member whose Membership in a Circle has the leader role.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin, host

**Topic**:
A named subject from the fixed taxonomy. Members can state Interests in Topics, and a Circle can declare a Primary Topic.
_Avoid_: Category, interest (when referring to the taxonomy entry)

**Interest**:
A Topic a Member has stated they care about. A Member may have none.

**Primary Topic**:
The single Topic a Circle declares as its subject. A Circle may have none.
_Avoid_: Topic (unqualified, when the distinction from Tags matters), category

**Circle without a Primary Topic**:
A Circle with no declared subject from the Topic taxonomy.
_Avoid_: Affinity Circle (when only the absence of a Primary Topic is known)

**Tag**:
A free-form label on a Circle. A Tag need not be a Topic in the taxonomy.

**Peer**:
Another Member who shares the Member's industry or belongs to a Circle the Member also belongs to.
_Avoid_: Similar user, neighbour, friend

**Cold-start Member**:
A Member with no stated Interests. The Member may still have Memberships and profile information.
_Avoid_: New user, empty profile, anonymous

**Affinity Circle**:
A Circle organised around a shared identity or region.
_Avoid_: Topicless Circle, uncategorised

### Eligibility

**Eligible Circle**:
A Circle the Member does not already belong to, and that is not Full, Unlisted, or Dormant.

**Full**:
A Circle whose member count equals or exceeds its capacity. A Circle with no capacity limit is never Full.
_Avoid_: At capacity, closed

**Unlisted**:
A Circle excluded from public discovery.
_Avoid_: Private, hidden

**Dormant**:
A Circle with no recorded activity for a long, fixed period.
_Avoid_: Inactive, dead, stale

**Quiet**:
A Circle with low recent activity that has not reached dormancy.
_Avoid_: Inactive

**Reference Time**:
The moment used to measure the age of Circle activity.
_Avoid_: Now, current time, today

**Request-to-join**:
A join policy that requires approval before a Member can join a Circle.
_Avoid_: Closed, gated, private

### Results

**Recommendation**:
One Eligible Circle proposed to a Member, with a Reason and any Alternatives.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, result, match

**Reason**:
The short, member-facing sentence that says why a Circle was recommended.
_Avoid_: Explanation, rationale, justification

**Alternative**:
An Eligible Circle on the same Primary Topic as a Recommendation, offered as another choice rather than listed on its own.
_Avoid_: Duplicate, runner-up, similar Circle
